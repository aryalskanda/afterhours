# Demo video: 75 seconds

**One idea:** you queue work at night from your phone; in the morning it's done, even though you hit your usage limit.

**Record it:**
- The phone side: screen-record the iPhone.
- The Mac side: QuickTime screen recording, with the menu bar and terminal font at a readable size.
- Cut it to 75 seconds with captions, since most people watch muted.

| Time | Picture | Caption or voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | Laptop closing on a desk at night, phone in hand | "It's 11 PM. My Claude Code work isn't done." |
| 5–15 s | Phone: type "Add CSV export to reports" and pick the folder → Queue | "I line up the next tasks from my phone." |
| 15–22 s | Two more tasks queued; the queue list shows 3 | "They run one after another on my Mac." |
| 22–32 s | Phone: "📝 Plan approved" with the plan text | "Claude plans first. Afterhours approves the plan, or sends it to me." |
| 32–40 s | Phone: "🔐 Claude wants to run npm test" → tap Allow | "Permissions come to my phone. If I'm asleep, it's declined and Claude finds another way." |
| 40–50 s | Phone: "⏸ hit the 5-hour limit · resets 3:30 AM" → clock jumps → "▶️ continued" | "When I hit my usage limit, it waits for the reset and carries on." |
| 50–62 s | Morning: phone shows three "✅ done" messages with diff stats | "In the morning, it's done: three tasks, each on its own branch." |
| 62–70 s | Mac: `git log` of the branch, merge | "I review and merge over coffee." |
| 70–75 s | Logo, the site's address | "Afterhours. Your Mac keeps coding while you're away." |

**Rules:**
- Show only real runs: no faked times, and no claims the product can't back.
- Keep "Claude Code" in plain text. No Anthropic logos.
- End with: "Not made by or affiliated with Anthropic."
