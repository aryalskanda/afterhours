How it works

  1. Install it on your Mac. Afterhours runs the real Claude Code, signed in with your own Claude plan, in the background.
  2. Pair your phone. Scan a code once. Your phone and your Mac talk end to end encrypted.
  3. Send tasks. Type what you need, pick the folder, and go. You get a message when it needs you and when it's done.
  • Your code stays on your Mac. Claude Code talks to Anthropic as it always does.
  • Afterhours never sees your Claude sign-in.
  • Nothing in between can read your prompts: only your phone and your Mac hold the keys.

The phone app and the encrypted connection are part of Pro, now in beta. The free version talks to your phone through your own Telegram bot.

Works alongside Claude's own tools

Remote Control is great for steering one live chat from the Claude app. Afterhours is for the work you line up and walk away from: a queue that runs through the night, waits out limits, and keeps going without you.

Questions

Is this allowed by Anthropic's terms?

Afterhours runs the unmodified Claude Code, signed in with your own Claude subscription or API key, on your own Mac. It never handles your credentials and never resells usage. Your usage counts against your own plan, as if you ran the tasks yourself.

Can Afterhours see my code or prompts?

Everything runs on your Mac. With Pro, messages between your phone and your Mac are end-to-end encrypted, so the relay in between only passes along what it can't read. The free version uses your own Telegram bot, whose messages pass through Telegram.

What happens when I hit my usage limit?

The running task waits for the reset and continues by itself. No new task starts until then. A chat you left open in VS Code is continued too.

Do I need a Mac?

Yes, for now: Afterhours runs on macOS 13 or newer, next to Claude Code. The phone app works on iPhone and Android.

Is Afterhours made by Anthropic?

No. It's an independent tool that works with Claude Code, and it isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
